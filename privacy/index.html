Privacy

Tivexa Privacy

Effective 8 November 2026. This policy covers the tivexa.games website and the playable browser versions of our games. Each Android app has its own policy, listed below.

The short version

We do not collect personal information through this website. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no cookies, no analytics and no tracking, and we run no server of our own. The browser versions of our games show no ads.

This website

tivexa.games is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub, as the host, may process technical information such as your IP address in its server logs, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We do not receive those logs, and we add no cookies, analytics or tracking of our own.

Playing a game in your browser

Some of our games can be played here without installing anything. Those browser versions:

  • show no ads. They contain no advertising or analytics service and contact no ad network;
  • keep your progress in your own browser's local storage, on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it;
  • have no account and no sign-in.

Clearing this site's data in your browser erases that saved progress. Progress in a browser version is separate from progress in the Android app — they do not sync.

The Android apps are different

Our Android apps are free and supported by ads from Google AdMob, which collects some data when an ad is requested and shown. That does not apply to this website or to the browser versions. Each app states exactly which ad formats it uses and what Google collects, in its own policy:

If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. We use it only to reply and to fix the problem you report. We do not sell it or share it with anyone, apart from our email provider, which stores our mailbox. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Each game's own policy states the age rating that applies to that game.
