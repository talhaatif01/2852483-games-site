Sortwise Privacy Policy

Effective 18 October 2026. Applies to the Sortwise app for Android and to this website.

Who we are

Sortwise is developed and published by 2852483 Alberta Inc., Alberta, Canada ("we", "us"). For any privacy question or request, email admin.tivexa@protonmail.com.

Summary

Sortwise does not collect personal information and does not send any data from your device to us or to anyone else. It has no account or sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no in-app purchases, and we operate no server for it. Your game progress stays on your device.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, Sortwise stores the following in its own private storage on your device:

  • which levels you have solved, with your best medal and best move count for each;
  • which introductions you have already seen (the tutorial and the cards that explain new rules);
  • whether sound is on or off.

This is kept in the app's private storage (its web storage and a private Android settings file), which other apps cannot read. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling Sortwise or by clearing its storage in your device settings (Settings > Apps > Sortwise > Storage > Clear storage). Deleting it resets your progress.

What Sortwise does not collect

Sortwise does not collect or access your name, email address, contacts, location, photos or files, camera, microphone, device identifiers or advertising ID. It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software (SDKs).

Permissions and internet

The app includes Android's standard Internet permission, which is part of the app framework Sortwise is built with. Sortwise's own code does not send or receive data over the internet, and the game plays fully offline. The How to play screen contains a link to this policy and an email link for support; if you tap one, it opens in your own browser or email app, outside Sortwise, and that app's own privacy terms apply. Sortwise requests no other permissions that give access to personal information.

Android backup

If you have turned on backup on your Android device, Android may include Sortwise's stored progress in your own device backup (for example, your Google account backup) and restore it when you set up a new device. Backups are made and stored by Android and your backup provider under their own terms. We cannot access them.

Google Play

Sortwise is distributed through Google Play. When you download or update it, Google processes information such as your Google account and device details under the Google Privacy Policy; this is Google's processing, not ours. If you have allowed your device to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, Google may give us crash and performance statistics for Sortwise in the Google Play Console. We use them only to find and fix problems, and they do not include your name or contact details.

If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. We use it only to reply and to fix the problem you report. We do not sell it or share it with anyone, apart from our email provider, which stores our mailbox. We keep support emails only as long as needed to handle your request, and you can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

Children

Sortwise does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Security

Because Sortwise does not transmit your data, your progress never leaves your device through the app. It is protected by Android's app sandbox, which keeps it private to Sortwise.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not use cookies, analytics or tracking on it. GitHub, as the host, may process technical information such as your IP address in its server logs under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Changes to this policy

If Sortwise's data practices change, for example if a future version added advertising or purchases, we will update this policy before releasing that version and show the new effective date at the top of this page.
