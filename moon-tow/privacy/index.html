Moon Tow Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026 (version 1.0.0 and later). Applies to the Moon Tow app for Android (package com.tivexa.moontow).

Moon Tow: Cargo Time Attack ("the app") is published by Tivexa Games, a trade name of 2852483 Alberta Inc.

What we collect

We do not collect personal information. The app has no account and no analytics of its own, and we run no servers that receive data from it.

Ads by Google AdMob

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob: a banner on the menu, level list and results screens; a full-screen ad between levels from the results screen; a full-screen ad when you open the app again after a long break (at most once every four hours, never in your first session); and an optional rewarded video you can choose to watch to keep your Daily Run streak after a missed day. Before any ad is requested, the app asks for your consent where the law requires it, using Google's consent tool (User Messaging Platform). You can change your choice at any time from "Privacy options" in the menu, where it applies.

To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, and app interaction and diagnostic data, as described in Google's policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings.

In-app purchases

"Remove ads" (one-time) and "Save my streak" (single-use) are optional purchases through Google Play. Google Play processes the payment; we receive only a confirmation that the purchase exists, which the app stores on your device. After "Remove ads", the banner, the ads between levels and the comeback ad stop; the optional rewarded video stays available. "Restore purchases" re-checks your purchases with Google Play.

Permissions

The app uses network access (internet and network state) only for ads, consent and Google Play purchases, and the advertising ID permission for ads. Flying plays fully offline.

What stays on your device

Your unlocked levels, best times, stars and best-run ghosts, your Daily Run best and streak, your settings (music, sound, ghost and control style) and the record of your purchases are stored only in the app's storage on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them (a "Remove ads" purchase can be restored from Google Play).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Ads are requested with Google's content rating limited to general audiences.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
