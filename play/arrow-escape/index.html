HOW TO PLAY

Free every arrow

Drag anywhere on the play area to turn the cube any way you like; Reset view recentres it. Tap a block to send it along its arrow once its path is clear. Your lives are one bank that carries from level to level: you start with 10, each blocked tap uses one, and every completed level adds one. When the bank is empty you can restart the level for free, or watch an ad for one extra life. No hints: look around every side.
