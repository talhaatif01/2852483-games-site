Night Shift Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026 (version 1.0.0 and later). Applies to the Night Shift app for Android (package com.tivexa.nightshift).

Effective 13 November 2026 (version 1.0.0 and later). Applies to the Night Shift app for Android (package com.tivexa.nightshift), published by Tivexa Games (2852483 Alberta Inc.).

What the app collects

The game itself collects nothing and has no servers. The game page makes no network connections. The app requests internet access only for Google's ads, consent and Play Billing (see below).

What the app stores on your device

Two best scores (one for Standard, one for Fast speed), your Controls choice (controller or touch), your Sound and Music settings, your chosen theme and your lives balance. They are kept in the app's private storage on your device only. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app removes them.

Ads and analytics

Ads (Google AdMob). Night Shift shows ads from Google AdMob: an optional rewarded ad for one extra life, a banner on the menu and shift-over screens and in its own space under the play area during a shift (never covering the game), and a full-screen ad on the shift-over screen after each finished shift except the first two of a session, at least 60 seconds apart and never during play or right after a purchase. Before any ad is requested, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form where the law requires it, and you can change it any time under "Privacy options" in the menu. Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from your IP address), ad interactions and diagnostics to show and measure ads; see Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy. The game itself does not collect these data.

In-app purchases (Google Play). You can buy a pack of 10 lives and a one-time "Remove ads" purchase. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play; the game never sees your payment details. Purchase records are kept by Google Play; on your device the game keeps only the Remove ads entitlement and the codes of lives packs it has already added, so a pack is never added twice.

There is no analytics SDK of our own and no account.

Offline

The game works fully offline. The Privacy policy, Terms and Support links in the game open this website in your browser.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version changes what the app stores or sends, this page will be updated before that version is released.
