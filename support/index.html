Questions about every game

Do I need an internet connection or an account? No. Our games have no account and no sign-in, and they play offline. Only the optional ads need a connection.

Where is my progress saved? Only on your device, in the app's own private storage. We cannot see it or restore it for you.

Are there in-app purchases? No. None of our games sell anything.

Can I play without ads? Every game can be finished without watching a single ad. Nothing in a game is locked behind one. Where your region offers it, you can also turn ads off from the game's "Ads and privacy choices" screen.

Sortwise

How do I reset my progress? "Reset all progress" in Options, or Settings > Apps > Sortwise > Storage > Clear storage. This deletes all medals and cannot be undone.

How do I see the tutorial again? Open How to play on the chapter list and tap Replay the tutorial.

Does Sortwise have ads? No purchases. Each level has 5 free hints; when they are used up you can choose to watch an ad to earn 1 more hint for that level (version 1.2.0 and later). A small banner is also shown at the bottom of the menu screens (chapter list, chapter screens, How to play), never during a puzzle or the tutorial. There are no full-screen ads.

How do I change my ad privacy choices? Open How to play and tap Privacy choices (shown where your region requires it).

Will my progress move to a new device? If Android backup is on, Android may restore it on a new device.

Read the Sortwise Privacy Policy.

Signal Garden

How do I reset my progress? "Reset all progress" in Options, or Settings > Apps > Signal Garden > Storage > Clear storage. This cannot be undone.

Does Signal Garden have ads? Only a small banner below the puzzle. There are no ads between levels, no full-screen ads, no rewarded ads and no in-app purchases.

How do I change my ad privacy choices? Open the app's "Ads and privacy choices" screen, where you can also turn the banner off. The game and all hints work the same with ads off.

Will my progress move to a new device? If Android backup is on, Android may restore it on a new device.

Read the Signal Garden Privacy Policy.

Number Forge

How do I reset my progress? "Reset progress" in the Menu, or Settings > Apps > Number Forge > Storage > Clear storage. This cannot be undone.

Does Number Forge have ads? A banner below the board, separated from the game controls; an occasional full-screen interstitial ad that appears only after about three minutes of active play, with a 10-second on-screen notice first; and an optional rewarded ad you can choose to watch. There are no hints for sale, rewarded ads are always optional, and Restart is always available.

How do I change my ad privacy choices? Open the app's "Ads and privacy choices" screen, where your region offers it. The game plays the same with ads off.

Will my progress move to a new device? If Android backup is on, Android may restore it on a new device.

Read the Number Forge Privacy Policy.

Arrow Escape

How do I reset my progress? "Reset progress" in the Menu, or Settings > Apps > Arrow Escape > Storage > Clear storage. This cannot be undone.

Does Arrow Escape have ads? A banner below the cube, separated from the game controls; an occasional full-screen interstitial ad only at a natural level break after about three minutes of active play, with a 10-second on-screen notice first; and an optional rewarded ad for extra lives. There are no hints for sale, the only thing an ad can give you is extra lives, and restarting is always free.

How do I change my ad privacy choices? Open the app's "Ads and privacy choices" screen, where your region offers it. The game plays the same with ads off.

Will my progress move to a new device? If Android backup is on, Android may restore it on a new device.

Read the Arrow Escape Privacy Policy.

First Out

How do I reset my progress? "Reset progress" in the menu, or Settings > Apps > First Out > Storage > Clear storage. This cannot be undone.

Does First Out have ads? A banner below the puzzle area, separated from the game controls; an occasional full-screen ad between levels; and optional ads you choose to watch yourself. There are no in-app purchases. Every level, every hint and Undo work exactly the same whether or not you see or watch an ad.

How many hints do I get? Two per level.

How do I change my ad privacy choices? Open the app's "Ads and privacy choices" screen.

Will my progress move to a new device? No. First Out opts out of Android app backup, so your progress is not included in device backups and does not transfer to a new device.

Read the First Out Privacy Policy.
