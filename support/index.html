Common questions

Do I need an internet connection or an account? No. Sortwise plays offline and has no account. Only the optional ads for extra hints need a connection.

Where is my progress saved? Only on your device. We cannot see it or restore it for you. If Android backup is on, Android may restore it on a new device.

How do I reset my progress? Settings > Apps > Sortwise > Storage > Clear storage. This deletes all medals and cannot be undone.

How do I see the tutorial again? Open How to play on the chapter list and tap Replay the tutorial.

Does Sortwise have ads or purchases? No purchases. Each level has 5 free hints; when they are used up you can choose to watch an ad to earn 1 more hint for that level (version 1.2.0 and later). From version 1.3.0 a small banner is also shown at the bottom of some screens, never during the tutorial or over the game board.

How do I change my ad privacy choices? Open How to play and tap Privacy choices (shown where your region requires it). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.
