Support

Stuck on a level, found a bug or have an idea? Email us and we'll help.

Email: admin.tivexa@protonmail.com

It helps to include the game's name, your phone model, your Android version, the app version (Settings > Apps > the game > App details) and the level you were on.

Questions about every game

Do I need an account or an internet connection?
No account is needed, and the games play offline. Ads and purchases use a connection.
Are there ads?
Most of our games show ads. Rewarded videos are always optional. Some games also show full-screen ads between levels or after longer play. Each game's section below and its privacy policy describe exactly what it shows. Pocket Circuit has no ads.
Are there in-app purchases?
Some games offer optional purchases, such as removing ads. Google Play handles every payment, and you're only charged after you confirm it there. A "Remove ads" purchase can be restored from Google Play if you reinstall.
Where is my progress saved?
On your phone, in the app's own storage. We can't see it or restore it. Sortwise can be restored by Android backup if you have it turned on. Our other games are left out of backup, so their progress stays on the phone where you played.
How do I reset a game?
Most games have a reset option in their menu. You can also go to Settings > Apps > the game > Storage > Clear storage. This can't be undone.
How do I change my ad privacy choices?
Open the game's privacy screen. Depending on the game it is called "Privacy choices", "Privacy options" or "Ads and privacy choices", and it appears where your region requires it.

Forge Rush

Does Forge Rush have ads?
Yes. A banner on the menu and results screens (never during play), short full-screen "cooling break" ads during longer play, and optional rewarded videos. Buying "Remove ads" turns off the banner and full-screen ads. Rewarded videos stay optional.
What can I buy?
Optional purchases through Google Play: Remove ads, tool and redo packs, wealth packs, gem styles and themes. You are only charged after you confirm in Google Play.
What is the World Ranking?
A leaderboard we plan to add. In the current version it shows as "Coming soon". When it opens, joining will be optional.
Does it need the internet?
The game itself plays offline. Ads and purchases need a connection.

Forge Rush on Google Play · Forge Rush Privacy Policy

Pipeworks

Does Pipeworks have ads?
Yes. A banner on the menu, level list and results screens, a full-screen ad between boards, at most one ad when you come back after 4 hours or more (from your second session on), and an optional rewarded video for a hint.
What can I buy?
Optional purchases through Google Play: "Remove ads" (one-time), 5 hints for your hint bank, and "Save my streak" for the Daily Pipe.
Can I remove ads?
Yes. "Remove ads" is an optional one-time purchase. It stops the banner and full-screen ads. The optional rewarded hint stays available, and the purchase can be restored from Google Play.

Pipeworks on Google Play · Pipeworks Privacy Policy

Moon Tow

Does Moon Tow have ads?
Yes. A banner on the menu, level list and results screens, a full-screen ad between levels, a full-screen ad when you come back after a long break, and an optional rewarded video.
What can I buy?
"Remove ads" (one-time) and "Save my streak" (single use), both optional through Google Play. Remove ads stops the banner, the ads between levels and the comeback ad.

Moon Tow on Google Play · Moon Tow Privacy Policy

Signal Garden

Does Signal Garden have ads?
Only a small banner below the puzzle. You can turn it off from the app's "Ads and privacy choices" screen. There are no in-app purchases.

Signal Garden on Google Play · Signal Garden Privacy Policy

Sortwise

Does Sortwise have ads?
A banner on the menu screens and an optional rewarded video for one extra hint on a level. There are no ads during a puzzle and no full-screen ads.
Can I remove ads?
Yes, on the Google Play version. Version 1.3.7 adds one optional purchase, "Remove ads". It removes the banner for good. The optional rewarded video for an extra hint stays available. The purchase is restored automatically on a new install, or with Settings > Restore purchases.
How do I see the tutorial again?
Open How to play on the chapter list and tap Replay the tutorial.

Sortwise on Google Play · Sortwise Privacy Policy

Arrow Escape

Does Arrow Escape have ads?
A banner below the cube, an occasional full-screen ad at a level break after about three minutes of play (with a 10-second notice first), and an optional rewarded ad for extra lives. Restarting a level is always free.

Gridwake

Number Forge

Number Forge on Google Play · Number Forge Privacy Policy

First Out

Does First Out have ads?
A banner below the puzzle, an occasional full-screen ad between levels, and optional ads you choose to watch. There are no in-app purchases.
How many hints do I get?

Will my progress move to a new phone?
No. First Out is left out of Android app backup, so progress stays on the phone where you played.

Delete your data

To ask us to delete your data, email admin.tivexa@protonmail.com and tell us which game. We will delete the personal information we hold about you, such as your emails to us, within 30 days of receiving your request, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Our games keep your progress only on your phone. Uninstalling a game, or clearing its storage, deletes it. Data collected by Google's ad software is held by Google: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings and manage your data in your Google Account.
