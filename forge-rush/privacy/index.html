Forge Rush - Privacy Policy

Effective date: the date of first publication on Google Play.

Forge Rush ("the app") is published by Tivexa Games, a trade name of 2852483 Alberta Inc.

In short

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information, and we do not use it for marketing. The app has no sign-up, no email, no contacts access and no location access. The only thing the app itself sends to us is an optional World Ranking entry that you create, and it exists only so players can compete with each other.

World Ranking (optional)

The World Ranking is off until you choose to join it. When you join, you pick a nickname, an emoji and a ring colour. The app then stores one ranking entry with: that nickname, emoji and colour; your total wealth and best-run wealth from the game; the time of the last update; and a random ID created for the entry (Firebase anonymous sign-in, which needs no email or password). Please don't use your real name as your nickname.

Other players can see your nickname, emoji, colour and wealth scores on the ranking. Nothing else about you is shown or stored.

The ranking is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), which processes it for us as a service provider; Google may process the technical data needed to deliver that service, such as your IP address, under the Firebase privacy terms. We use the ranking only to show the leaderboard and your rank. We do not use it for ads or analytics.

If you report a nickname as unsuitable, the app stores the report (the reported entry's random ID, your entry's random ID and the time) so we can review it; the name is also hidden on your device.

Deleting your ranking data: tap "Delete my ranking data" on the World Ranking screen. This deletes your ranking entry and its random ID from our database straight away. You can also e-mail us to ask for deletion. We delete entries that have not been updated for 24 months.

Ads by Google AdMob

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the menu and results screens (never during play or the story film); a short "cooling break" ad, at most once every four minutes of active play, only right after you finish an objective (never in the middle of a puzzle), announced a few seconds ahead, and never during your first session; a full-screen ad at the end of some runs (not during your first session); a full-screen ad when you open the app or come back to it after four hours or more away, at most once every four hours and never on your first visit; and optional rewarded videos you can choose to watch for a free Glass Hammer, a redo, or double rewards. Buying "Remove ads" turns off the banner and all full-screen ads; rewarded videos stay optional. Before any ad is requested, the app asks for your consent where the law requires it, using Google's consent tool (User Messaging Platform). You can change your choice at any time from "Privacy options" in Settings, where it applies.

To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, and app interaction and diagnostic data, as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings. The app's own code does not collect this data.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases (remove ads, tool and redo packs, wealth packs, gem styles and themes) are made through Google Play. Google Play processes the payment; we receive only a confirmation that the purchase exists, which the app stores on your device.

Permissions

The app uses network access (internet and network state) only for ads, consent, Google Play purchases and the optional World Ranking, and the advertising ID permission for ads. The game itself plays offline.

What stays on your device

Your wealth bank, best scores, challenges, tools, gem styles, themes, settings, purchase records and simple play statistics (such as runs played, jams and continues used, kept for 30 days) are stored only in the app's storage on your device. The play statistics are never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them (purchases can be restored from Google Play). Your World Ranking entry, if you made one, stays until you delete it as described above.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and the World Ranking is not intended for them. Ads are requested with Google's content rating limited to general audiences. If you believe a child has created a ranking entry, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
