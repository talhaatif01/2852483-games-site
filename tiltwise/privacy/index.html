Tiltwise Privacy Policy

Effective 20 October 2026. Applies to the Tiltwise app for Android (version 1.0.0 and later).

Who we are

Tiltwise is a TIVEXA game, developed and published by 2852483 Alberta Inc., Alberta, Canada ("we", "us"). For any privacy question or request, email admin.tivexa@protonmail.com.

Summary

We (2852483 Alberta Inc.) do not collect personal information. Tiltwise has no account or sign-in, no analytics of our own, no in-app purchases, and we operate no server for it. Your game progress stays on your device.

Ads play only when you ask for one. Each level has 2 free hints; when they are used up you can choose to watch a rewarded ad, provided by Google AdMob, to unlock 1 extra hint for that level. There are no banner ads and no ads that appear by themselves. The game is fully playable without ever watching an ad.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress and settings, Tiltwise stores the following in its own private storage on your device:

  • which levels you have solved, with your best medal and best tilt count for each;
  • how many hints you have used and earned on each level, and a short list of identifiers of rewarded ads already credited (so a reward is never counted twice);
  • which introductions you have already seen (the tutorial and the cards that explain new rules);
  • your sound and direction-button settings.

This is kept in the app's private storage (its web storage and a private Android settings file), which other apps cannot read. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling Tiltwise or by clearing its storage in your device settings (Settings > Apps > Tiltwise > Storage > Clear storage). Deleting it resets your progress.

What we do not collect

We do not collect or access your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos or files, camera or microphone. Tiltwise contains no analytics or tracking software of its own. The only third-party software in Tiltwise that can collect data is Google's advertising and consent software described below.

Ads and consent

Which ads. Only rewarded ads, and only when you ask: on a level whose free hints are used up, tap "Need a nudge?" and then "Watch ad". There are no banner ads, no ads between levels, no full-screen ads you did not ask for, and no in-app purchases.

When Google's software contacts Google. Each time the app starts, Google's consent software (the User Messaging Platform) asks Google whether a consent message applies in your region. Google's advertising software starts only when you tap "Watch ad"; it then requests and shows an ad.

Who and what. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited, depending on your region). When an ad is requested and shown, Google's software collects and shares, for advertising, measurement (analytics) and fraud prevention:

  • your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
  • device and app identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • interactions with the ad (for example that it was shown, watched, closed or tapped) and app launch information;
  • diagnostic information such as performance and load times.

Google's software encrypts this data in transit. How Google uses it is explained in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy. Depending on the consent choices you make, Google and its advertising partners may show you personalized or non-personalized ads. We set Google's ad content limit to a teen rating, because Tiltwise is meant for players aged 13 and over.

Consent and your choices. Where the law requires your consent (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), Google's consent form is shown the first time you tap "Watch ad", before any ad is requested. You can change your choices at any time with "Privacy choices" in How to play, where your region requires it. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

If no ad is shown. If you close an ad early, are offline, decline ads, or no ad is available, no hint is earned, nothing is used up, and you keep playing normally.

What we receive. We do not receive your advertising ID or other identifiers from these ads. We receive aggregated reports from Google about ad performance (for example how many ads were shown and the resulting earnings); they do not identify you.

Permissions and internet

Tiltwise's own game code does not send or receive data over the internet, and the game plays fully offline (only the optional rewarded ads need a connection). The app asks for no permissions while you play. It includes Android's Internet permission and the permissions Google's advertising software requires: network state, advertising ID, basic phone state (for example the type of network connection; it does not give access to your phone number or calls), and wake lock and foreground service for its background tasks. These are granted when the app is installed and do not give access to your contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or precise location.

Android backup

If you have turned on backup on your Android device, Android may include Tiltwise's stored progress in your own device backup (for example, your Google account backup) and restore it when you set up a new device. Backups are made and stored by Android and your backup provider under their own terms. We cannot access them.

Google Play

Tiltwise is distributed through Google Play. When you download or update it, Google processes information such as your Google account and device details under the Google Privacy Policy; this is Google's processing, not ours. If you have allowed your device to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, Google may give us crash and performance statistics for Tiltwise in the Google Play Console. We use them only to find and fix problems, and they do not include your name or contact details.

If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. We use it only to reply and to fix the problem you report. We do not sell it or share it with anyone, apart from our email provider, which stores our mailbox. We keep support emails only as long as needed to handle your request, and you can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

Children

Tiltwise is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Security

Your progress never leaves your device through the game itself. It is protected by Android's app sandbox, which keeps it private to Tiltwise. Data collected by Google's advertising software is handled by Google as described above.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not use cookies, analytics or tracking on it. GitHub, as the host, may process technical information such as your IP address in its server logs under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Changes to this policy

If Tiltwise's data practices change, we will update this policy and the Play listing's Data safety answers before releasing that version, and show the new effective date at the top of this page.
