Our game

Tiltwise

Tiltwise is a calm puzzle game for Android about one simple move: tilt the board. Every marble and crate rolls at the same time until it hits something, and each marble must drop into the hole of its own colour.

  • 82 puzzles in 8 chapters, with a short hands-on tutorial.
  • Every level is proven solvable; Gold means you found the fewest possible tilts.
  • Free, unlimited Undo and Restart, and the game tells you when a position can no longer be finished.
  • Swipe to tilt; optional on-screen direction buttons. Colours also have shapes.
  • Each level has 2 free hints. When they run out you can choose to watch an ad to unlock 1 extra hint. Ads never play unless you ask; there are no banner ads.
  • Free. No in-app purchases, no account. Plays offline. Your progress is saved on your device only.

Tiltwise is being prepared for release on Google Play.

Common questions

I made a wrong tilt and can't finish the level. Tap Undo (as many times as you like) or Restart. Both are always free.

How do I get more hints? Each level has 2 free hints. After that, tap "Need a nudge?" and choose "Watch ad" to unlock 1 extra hint for that level. If the ad doesn't finish or isn't available, nothing is used up.

How do I change my ad privacy choices? Open How to play and tap "Privacy choices" (shown where your region requires it).

How do I reset my progress? Clear Tiltwise's storage in Settings > Apps > Tiltwise > Storage, or uninstall the app. This deletes your progress permanently.
