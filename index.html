About Tivexa

We're a small, independent game studio in Canada. We make games you can open for a few minutes on a break: puzzles with one clear rule to learn, and a few small arcade games. Every level is tested before it ships, many by a solver that checks it can be finished.

Our games are free on Google Play. Most show ads, and some offer optional purchases such as removing ads. Each game's store page and privacy policy spell out exactly what it does.
