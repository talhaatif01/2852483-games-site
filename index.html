About Tivexa

We're an independent game studio in Canada. We care most about how a game feels to play: one clear idea, levels that ramp up fairly, and art and sound you want to come back to.

Every level is tested before it ships, and many are checked by a solver to make sure they can be finished. Our games are free on Google Play. Most show ads, and some offer optional purchases such as removing them.

Found a bug or have an idea? Visit Support or email admin.tivexa@protonmail.com.
