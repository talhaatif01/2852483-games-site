Prism Foundry - Privacy Policy

Effective date: the date of first publication on Google Play.

Prism Foundry ("the app") is published by Tivexa Games, a trade name of 2852483 Alberta Inc.

In short

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information, and we do not use it for marketing. The app has no sign-up, no email, no contacts access and no location access. The app itself sends nothing to us.

Ads by Google AdMob

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob: a banner on menu screens (never on the puzzle board); a short full-screen "cooling break" ad, at most once every four minutes of active play, only right after you clear a station (never in the middle of a puzzle), and never during your first session; and optional rewarded videos you can choose to watch for a free Reforge or a free perk. Buying "Remove Ads" turns off the banner and all full-screen ads; rewarded videos stay optional. Before any ad is requested, the app asks for your consent where the law requires it, using Google's consent tool (User Messaging Platform). You can change your choice at any time from "Privacy options" in Settings, where it applies.

To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, and app interaction and diagnostic data, as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings. The app's own code does not collect this data.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases (Remove Ads, perk packs, Reforge packs, Streak Saver and gem styles) are made through Google Play. Google Play processes the payment; we receive only a confirmation that the purchase exists, which the app stores on your device.

Finisher certificate

If you clear all 150 stations, the app can make a certificate and a proof-of-completion code from the moves you played. They are created on your device and are only shared if you choose to share them yourself, using your phone's own share menu.

Permissions

The app uses network access (internet and network state) only for ads, consent and Google Play purchases, and the advertising ID permission for ads. The game itself plays offline.

What stays on your device

Your stations, stars, perks, gem styles, settings, purchase records, finisher records and simple play statistics (such as sessions played and stations retried) are stored only in the app's storage on your device. The play statistics are never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them (purchases can be restored from Google Play).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Ads are requested with Google's content rating limited to general audiences.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
