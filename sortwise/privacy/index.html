Sortwise Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026 (version 1.3.7 and later). Applies to the Sortwise app for Android. The previous version of this policy (effective 6 November 2026) applied to versions 1.3.6 and earlier.

Who we are

Sortwise is a TIVEXA game, developed and published by 2852483 Alberta Inc., Alberta, Canada ("we", "us"). For any privacy question or request, email admin.tivexa@protonmail.com.

Summary

Sortwise has no account or sign-in, no gameplay analytics of our own, and no server of ours, so what you do in the game is not sent to us. Your game progress stays on your device. We receive personal information in only two ways: if you buy Remove ads, we can see the order record in Google Play Console, and if you email us, we receive your message. Some information is processed by Google services built into the app: Google AdMob and Google's consent tool for ads (see Ads) and, on the Google Play version, Google Play Billing for the one optional purchase, Remove ads (see In-app purchase).

Sortwise is free and supported by a banner ad on the menu screens, provided by Google AdMob, which collects some data when ads are requested and shown (see Ads). Each level has free hints; when they are used up you can choose to watch an optional rewarded ad for one more. Watching is always your choice, and the game can be completed without watching any ad.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, Sortwise stores the following in its own private storage on your device:

  • which levels you have completed, with your best medal and move count for each;
  • the current state of any unfinished level and your move count;
  • how many hints you have used on each level, including any earned by watching a rewarded ad;
  • your sound and vibration settings;
  • on the Google Play version, whether you own Remove ads, the purchase token Google Play returns for it, and when this was last checked (see In-app purchase).

This is kept in the app's private storage (its web storage and a private Android settings file), which other apps cannot read. The app does not send it to us. The only part that leaves your device is the Remove ads check with Google Play, described under In-app purchase. "Reset all progress" in Settings erases the game save (your sound setting and privacy choices are kept); uninstalling or clearing the app's storage (Settings > Apps > Sortwise > Storage > Clear storage) also removes it.

What we do not collect

We do not collect or access your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos or files, camera or microphone. Sortwise contains no analytics or tracking software of its own. The only third-party software in the app that can collect data is Google's advertising and consent software and, on the Google Play version, Google Play's billing software, all described below.

Ads

Which ads. A banner at the bottom of the menu screens, never during a puzzle or the tutorial, and an optional rewarded video you can choose to watch for one more hint when a level's free hints are used up. There are no full-screen ads between levels and no ads during play. If you buy Remove ads, the banner is no longer requested or shown. Watching a rewarded ad is always your choice — every level can be solved without one.

Who. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited, depending on your region). When an ad is requested and shown, Google's software collects and shares, for advertising, measurement (analytics) and fraud prevention:

  • your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
  • device and app identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • interactions with the ad (for example that it was shown, closed or tapped) and app launch information;
  • diagnostic information such as performance and load times.

Google's software encrypts this data in transit. How Google uses it is explained in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy; the SDK's data practices are listed at Google's AdMob data disclosure. Depending on the consent choices you make, Google and its advertising partners may show you personalized or non-personalized ads. We set Google's ad content limit to a teen rating, because Sortwise is meant for players aged 13 and over.

Consent and your choices. Sortwise uses Google's consent software (the User Messaging Platform). The app asks Google whether consent is needed in your region; this request goes to Google. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area), Google asks for your choice before an ad is shown. If you decline, ads can still appear, served according to your choice. Where your region requires it, you can change your choice at any time with "Privacy choices" in Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Changing your choice does not delete data Google has already received. If no ad is available, for example when you are offline, the game tells you and you keep playing.

What we receive. We do not receive your advertising ID or other identifiers from these ads. We receive aggregated reports from Google about ad performance (for example how many ads were shown and the resulting earnings); they do not identify you.

In-app purchase

What you can buy. The Google Play version of Sortwise (1.3.7 and later) offers one optional, one-time purchase: Remove ads. It removes the banner ad permanently. The optional rewarded video for an extra hint stays available, so you can still choose to watch one. Nothing else is sold. Versions of Sortwise distributed outside Google Play have no purchase.

Who processes the payment. The purchase is made through Google Play's own purchase screen and processed by Google Play Billing under Google's terms. Your payment details are handled by Google, and we do not receive your card details. Like every developer, we can see records of orders for our apps in Google Play Console, which we use only to manage purchases, refunds and support requests.

What the app receives and stores. Through Google Play's billing software on your device, the app receives whether Remove ads is owned, its purchase state and a purchase token. It stores only whether Remove ads is owned, that token and the time it was last checked, in its private storage on your device. To complete the purchase and confirm ownership, the app exchanges this purchase information, including the token, with Google Play Billing; Google Play processes it, and keeps its own record of the purchase in your Google account, under Google's terms. The app does not send this information to us or to anyone other than Google Play.

Restoring. Each time Sortwise starts or returns to the foreground, it asks Google Play whether your Google account owns Remove ads. That is how the purchase comes back after a reinstall or on a new device signed in to the same Google account. You can also tap Restore purchases in Settings. If Google Play reports that the purchase was refunded, ads come back. If Google Play can't be reached, the last known result on your device is used.

Refunds. Refunds are handled under Google Play's refund policies. You can also email us at the address below.

Permissions and internet

Sortwise's own game code does not send or receive data over the internet, and the game plays fully offline. The app includes Android's Internet and network-state permissions, which Google's advertising software requires (on the Google Play version, Google Play's billing software also uses the device's connection to Google Play to make and check the Remove ads purchase), and the vibration permission for optional haptic feedback. These do not give access to your contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or precise location. The game's web view loads only files bundled inside the app.

Android backup

Sortwise allows Android app backup. If backup is switched on for your device, Android may include the app's saved data (your game save, and on the Google Play version whether you own Remove ads, which the app re-checks with Google Play) and restore it when you set up a new device. This is handled by Android and your Google account, not by us — we never see it. You can control it in your device's backup settings.

Google Play

Sortwise is distributed through Google Play. When you download or update it, Google processes information such as your Google account and device details under the Google Privacy Policy; this is Google's processing, not ours. If you have allowed your device to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, Google may give us crash and performance statistics in the Google Play Console. We use them only to find and fix problems, and they do not include your name or contact details.

If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. We use it only to reply and to fix the problem you report. We do not sell it or share it with anyone, apart from our email provider, which stores our mailbox. We keep support emails only as long as needed to handle your request, and you can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

Children

Sortwise is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. The game itself does not ask for or send us personal information. We may receive personal information only through a Google Play order record, if Remove ads is bought, or an email sent to us. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if you believe a child has contacted us, email us and we will delete the message. Google's ad software collects the information described under Ads; ad content is limited to a teen rating.

Your rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing the address under Contact. For Sortwise we normally hold none, apart from any email you send us and the order records Google Play Console shows us if you buy Remove ads. For data that Google collects through its ad, consent and billing software, use Google's own tools (for example your Google Account and Android ad settings). If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or the privacy authority where you live.

Security

Your progress never leaves your device through the game itself. It is protected by Android's app sandbox, which keeps it private to Sortwise. Data collected by Google's advertising software is handled by Google as described above.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not use cookies, analytics or tracking on it. GitHub, as the host, may process technical information such as your IP address in its server logs under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Changes to this policy

If Sortwise's data practices change, we will update this policy before releasing that version and show the new effective date at the top of this page.
