Gridwake Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026 (version 1.0.0 and later). Applies to the Gridwake app for Android (package com.tivexa.gridwake).

Who we are

Gridwake is a TIVEXA game, developed and published by 2852483 Alberta Inc., Alberta, Canada ("we", "us"). For any privacy question or request, email admin.tivexa@protonmail.com.

Summary

We do not collect personal information. Gridwake has no account or sign-in, no analytics, no ads, no in-app purchases, and we operate no server for it. The game plays fully offline and your progress stays on your device.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, Gridwake stores the following in its own private storage on your device:

  • which of the 50 puzzles you have completed, and which chapters are unlocked;
  • the state of the grid in a puzzle you have not finished;
  • how many hints you have used on each puzzle (two per puzzle maximum);
  • whether you have finished the tutorial;
  • whether sound, music and vibration are on or off.

This is kept in the app's private storage (its web storage inside the app's sandbox), which other apps cannot read. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage (Settings > Apps > Gridwake > Storage > Clear storage) removes it.

What we do not collect

We do not collect or access your name, email address, contacts, location, photos or files, camera or microphone. Gridwake contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software, and no third-party software that collects data.

Permissions and internet

Gridwake does not send or receive data over the internet and has no internet permission. Its only Android permission is vibration, used for optional haptic feedback that you can turn off in the menu. The game's web view loads only files bundled inside the app. The menu contains links to this website, which open in your browser only when you tap them.

Android backup

Gridwake opts out of Android app backup, so your progress is not included in device backups and does not transfer to a new device.

Google Play

Gridwake is distributed through Google Play. When you download or update it, Google processes information such as your Google account and device details under the Google Privacy Policy; this is Google's processing, not ours. If you have allowed your device to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, Google may give us crash and performance statistics in the Google Play Console. We use them only to find and fix problems, and they do not include your name or contact details.

If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. We use it only to reply and to fix the problem you report. We do not sell it or share it with anyone, apart from our email provider, which stores our mailbox. We keep support emails only as long as needed to handle your request, and you can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

Children

Gridwake is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Security

Your progress never leaves your device. It is protected by Android's app sandbox, which keeps it private to Gridwake.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not use cookies, analytics or tracking on it. GitHub, as the host, may process technical information such as your IP address in its server logs under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Changes to this policy

If Gridwake's data practices change (for example if a later version adds ads), we will update this policy before releasing that version and show the new effective date at the top of this page.
