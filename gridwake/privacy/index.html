Gridwake Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026 (version 1.1.0 and later). Applies to the Gridwake app for Android (package com.tivexa.gridwake).

Gridwake ("the app") is a picture logic puzzle published by Tivexa Games, a trade name of 2852483 Alberta Inc.

What we collect

We do not collect personal information. The app has no account and no sign-in, no analytics of its own, and we run no servers that receive data from it. Your solved puzzles, moves, the puzzle you are working on, hints used and your sound, music and vibration settings are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.

Ads by Google AdMob

The app is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob: a banner on the menu, the level select and the results card; a full-screen ad after a solved puzzle (never after the first two of a session, at least a minute apart, never during a puzzle); at most one full-screen ad when you open the app after being away for four hours or more (never in your first session); and an optional video you can choose to watch for one extra hint. Ads are never shown on a puzzle screen.

Before any ad is requested, the app asks for your consent where the law requires it, using Google's consent tool (User Messaging Platform). You can change your choice at any time from "Privacy options" in the Menu, where it applies. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, and app interaction and diagnostic data, as described in Google's policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings.

In-app purchases

"Remove ads" is an optional one-time purchase through Google Play. "5 hints" adds five hints to a hint bank used after a puzzle's two free hints, kept across puzzles, and can be bought again. Google Play processes every payment; we receive only a confirmation that the purchase exists, which the app stores on your device. After "Remove ads", the banner and full-screen ads (including the app-open ad) stop; the optional rewarded hint stays available. "Restore purchases" in the Menu re-checks your purchases with Google Play.

Permissions

The app uses network access (internet and network state) only for ads, consent and Google Play purchases, and the advertising ID permission for ads. Puzzles play fully offline.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Ads are requested with Google's content rating limited to general audiences.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not use cookies, analytics or tracking on it. GitHub, as the host, may process technical information such as your IP address in its server logs under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
